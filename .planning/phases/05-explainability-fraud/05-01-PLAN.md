# PLAN 05-01 — Findings with provenance, audit trail, cited summaries, fraud signals, pulse-liveness, anomalies

## Objective

Make every system conclusion explainable: deterministic finding generators emit full provenance (artifact ids, rule/model, confidence, evidence snippet) behind an NFR-02 hard gate (API validation + UI suppression of provenance-less findings); an append-only audit trail records all case actions; Gemini Flash writes case summaries with inline `[artifact:<id>]` citations that degrade gracefully offline; media gets ELA + EXIF tamper flags, a lazy-loaded deepfake score with heuristic fallback, and video faces get an rPPG pulse-liveness signal (FRD-03) ported from the working pulse-detector prototype; log sessions get IsolationForest anomaly scores with contributing features.

### Task 5.6 — Pulse-liveness rPPG for video faces (FRD-03)
- **read_first:** `~/workspace/pulse-detector/pulse_detector.py` (port `detrend`, `bandpass`, `estimate_bpm`, and the ROI approach — do not re-derive), `phases/05-explainability-fraud/05-CONTEXT.md`
- **action:**
  1. Create `apps/api/app/services/pulse.py`: verbatim-port the DSP core (`detrend`, `bandpass`, `estimate_bpm`); add `analyze_video(path)` — OpenCV frame read, every 3rd frame, mediapipe `FaceMesh` → landmark bounding box → forehead/cheek ROIs (same fractions as prototype) → green-channel mean series → `estimate_bpm`. Requires ≥ 5 s of face-tracked signal; fewer → `pulse_verdict="insufficient-signal"`, no finding.
  2. Verdict mapping (calibrated): SNR ≥ 12 → `real-face-supported`; 6–12 → `inconclusive`; < 6 → `possibly-synthetic`; no face in enough frames → `no-face`. Confidence = `clip(snr/20, 0.05, 0.95)` for directional verdicts, `0.5` for inconclusive. Store `pulse_bpm/pulse_snr/pulse_verdict` on the `media_signals` row.
  3. Add `POST /api/media/{id}/pulse` (rejects non-video artifacts with 422); findings rule `pulse-liveness` emits a finding with artifact id, method `rPPG remote photoplethysmography`, SNR, confidence, and the "supporting signal, not proof" caveat in the description.
  4. Add `opencv-python`, `mediapipe`, `numpy`, `scipy` to requirements (CPU-only).
  5. Fixtures: a synthetic "real pulse" video (Pillow/OpenCV-generated face-like patch with a 72 BPM green-channel modulation — the prototype's self-test signal rendered as frames) and a flat/no-pulse video.
- **verify:** `POST /api/media/{id}/pulse` on the pulsing fixture → `pulse_snr ≥ 12`, verdict `real-face-supported`, BPM within ±3 of 72; on the flat fixture → verdict `possibly-synthetic` (or `inconclusive`), never `real-face-supported`; a 2-second clip → `insufficient-signal` and no finding.
- **acceptance_criteria:**
  - Pulsing fixture yields SNR ≥ 12 and BPM ≈ 72 (±3); flat fixture never yields `real-face-supported`; short clip yields `insufficient-signal` with zero finding rows; every emitted finding passes the NFR-02 gate (artifact id + method + SNR + confidence present).
